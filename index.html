Behind the edit

I'm Prashant, a video editor and creative media specialist with experience in educational, promotional, interview and social media content.

My journey started with a simple phone, photographing anything I could see. I failed many times and learned most things by doing, and I took feedback from other professionals along the way.

I began with photography. As my passion grew, I decided to take the next step and work professionally as a videographer too. I learned angles and shots by watching movies and other professionals.

I still believe there is always room to grow and learn.
